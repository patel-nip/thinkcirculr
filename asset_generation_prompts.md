# Media Asset Generation Blueprint: "CIRCULR"
## Daylit, Organic & Humorous Image & Video Prompts for Gemini Models

This document outlines the detailed image generation prompts (for Gemini Image/Multimodal models) and video generation prompts (for Gemini Video/Omni models) to create the bright, organic, and lighthearted assets required for the **CIRCULR** interactive landing page.

---

## Part 1: Image Generation Prompts (Gemini / Image Models)
These prompts are optimized to generate static daylit product shots, unbleached textures, and witty micro-graphic overlays.

### 1. The Hero Cinematic Scene
*   **Purpose:** Background image fallback / parallax landscape layer.
*   **Prompt:** 
    > A cinematic, wide-angle 21:9 shot of a sun-drenched bamboo forest during early morning. Bright, warm sunbeams filtering through the tall green stalks, casting soft shadows on the ground. A gentle morning mist rising, cinematic depth of field, vibrant natural greens and warm gold tones. Clean, airy, and high-end organic aesthetic.

### 2. Product Pedestal: Bamboo Toilet Paper
*   **Purpose:** Section 4 product grid card asset.
*   **Prompt:** 
    > High-end daylit studio product photography of a premium 6-pack of unbleached bamboo toilet paper rolls wrapped in minimalist, pastel-pink recycled paper packaging with a clean forest-green brand logo. The pack is posed on a raw, light-colored wooden pedestal. High contrast, sharp details showing the soft, textured unbleached paper fibers, soft natural shadows, clean and modern eco-luxury style.

### 3. Product Pedestal: Bamboo Tissue Tubes
*   **Purpose:** Cup-holder tissue cylinders.
*   **Prompt:** 
    > Minimalist product shot of two sleek, cylinder-shaped bamboo tissue tubes side-by-side: one in a matte charcoal-grey, the other in a soft pastel-blue. Tissues are slightly pulling out of the top openings. Placed inside a clean, modern daylit car interior's cup holder, with bright morning sunlight streaming through the window and illuminating the soft matte textures of the cylinders.

### 4. Product Pedestal: Compostable Garbage Bags
*   **Purpose:** Waste disposal bags.
*   **Prompt:** 
    > A roll of compostable kitchen garbage bags made of organic cornstarch. The bags have a translucent, soft-matte sage green color. Placed on an earthy, light-beige ceramic tray in a bright, modern scandinavian kitchen. Soft, natural daylight, ultra-sharp focus on the organic texture of the starch-based material, clean and sustainable home care aesthetic.

### 5. The X-Ray Magnifier Scope: Cute Koala Secret
*   **Purpose:** Reveal texture and joke on the hover magnifier mask.
*   **Prompt:** 
    > A macro, extreme close-up view of raw, unbleached bamboo cellulose fibers woven together in a soft linen-like texture. In the center of the fibers, a tiny, cute, flat-design cartoon koala is sitting, holding a small wooden sign that says "Thanks for not cutting my tree!". Warm daylit macro lighting, soft depth of field, whimsical and organic texture.

### 6. Share Graphic Background: Pastel Eco-Leaf Gradient
*   **Purpose:** "Bum-Score" calculator share badge template.
*   **Prompt:** 
    > A clean, minimalist graphic background. A soft, warm cream to pastel-green gradient canvas. Delicate vector leaf silhouettes and bamboo shoots frame the border in a modern, organic design. High-quality vector illustration style, airy, clean, and inviting aesthetic.

## Part 2: Interactive Video Generation Prompts (Gemini Omni / Video Models)
These prompts are designed for video generators to produce clean, daylit loops, scroll-scrubbable sequences, and landing page transitions.

### 1. Section 1: The Widescreen Hook (Forest Loop)
*   **Purpose:** Background loop for the landing page hero section.
*   **Prompt:** 
    > A 10-second loop. A slow-motion, steady cinematic camera shot moving forward through a bright, sun-drenched bamboo forest. Morning dew drops glistening on the leaves. Volumetric sun rays cutting through the canopy, illuminating the leaves as they sway gently in a soft breeze. Bright natural greens, loopable, cinema quality, clean and peaceful atmosphere.

### 2. Section 2: Deforestation Tree-Pop Sequence
*   **Purpose:** Scrub-linked background transition showing trees popping into the ground.
*   **Prompt:** 
    > A 5-second clean 2D vector motion graphics animation in flat isometric perspective. On a soft warm cream (#FAF8F5) background, a neat grid of cute, simplified green cartoon pine and broadleaf trees is displayed. As the video progresses, the trees comically squash-and-stretch, pulling themselves down into the ground one-by-one in a rapid, satisfying sequence. Each disappearance triggers a tiny, playful "poof" explosion of white dust clouds and green leaf particles, leaving behind a smooth, clean clay soil texture. The animation is extremely fluid, incorporating professional cartoon physics, clean vector lines, and flat pastel colors. 60fps, crisp digital render.

### 3. Section 3: The Elastic Bamboo Growth Shoot
*   **Purpose:** Scrub-linked growth sequence in the solution section.
*   **Prompt:** 
    > A 5-second 3D animation. A single, stylized bright-green bamboo shoot sprouts from a tiny heap of earth. The bamboo grows upwards with comically springy, elastic stretching animations. At each segmented node, a pair of leaves pops open like a cartoon spring. Volumetric studio backlighting highlights the translucent green of the leaves, clean cream backdrop, fun and organic feel.

### 4. Section 4: Product Showcase Loops
*   **Purpose:** Autoplay muted video loops behind the interactive product cards.
*   **Prompt:** 
    > A 6-second hyper-realistic 3D product showcase loop. A single roll of unbleached bamboo toilet paper floats weightlessly in the center of the frame, rotating slowly 360-degrees on its vertical axis. The camera is locked in a close-up macro shot, capturing the detailed organic, raw fibrous paper texture. Underneath the roll, a shallow, crystal-clear water pool reflects soft green leaves from an overhead canopy. Gentle water ripples expand outward in slow-motion as the roll rotates. The background is a clean, bright studio backdrop in soft warm cream. High-key natural lighting with soft sunbeams creates a pure, premium organic feel. 4K resolution, 60fps, seamless looping.

### 5. Section 5: The Configurator Box Drop Action
*   **Purpose:** Interactive drop feedback video.
*   **Prompt:** 
    > A 3-second sequence. A clean, plain cardboard box drops onto a warm cream surface, opening dynamically with a satisfying, bouncy cartoon-like impact. From inside the box, a burst of paper leaf-shaped green confetti and cartoon sun emojis float upwards. Bouncy physics, bright daylit studio lighting, transparent background (alpha channel), clean 3D animation.

### 6. Bamboo-to-Product Process Demonstration
*   **Purpose:** A clear animation demonstrating how raw bamboo trees are processed and turned into final paper products.
*   **Prompt:**
    > An 8-second 3D isometric motion graphics sequence showcasing the process of converting raw bamboo into unbleached paper tissue rolls. On a soft warm cream (#FAF8F5) backdrop:
    > 1. Harvest & Crush: A mature green bamboo stalk is sliced length-wise into raw, pale-beige fiber chips.
    > 2. Pulping: The chips blend into a textured, organic water pulp inside a modern, cylindrical stainless-steel spinner.
    > 3. Pressing & Drying: The wet pulp is drawn out into thin, textured unbleached paper sheets passing through rolling heated rollers.
    > 4. Rolling: The dried sheets wind tightly onto a cardboard cylinder core, forming a finished, textured, unbleached 3-ply bamboo toilet paper roll.
    > The transitions are fluid and mechanical, showing a realistic step-by-step physical processing connection rather than magical illusions. Bright daylit studio lighting, clean line styling, and professional cartoon physics. 60fps, crisp digital render.
